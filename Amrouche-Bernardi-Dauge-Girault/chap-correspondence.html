9 Correspondence with the paper

  • Formalized: Theorem 3.12 (tangential vector potentials) and Theorem 3.17 (normal vector potentials), with the paper’s hypotheses on \(\Omega \) and on the cuts (Definition 2.3). Lean numbers the cuts \(0,\dots ,J-1\).

  • Function spaces. Fields are classes in \(L^2(\Omega )^3\), so uniqueness is uniqueness in \(L^2(\Omega )^3\). In the paper \(\psi \) ranges over \(X(\Omega )=H(\operatorname {curl},\Omega )\cap H(\operatorname {div},\Omega )\); this is the same, since \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\in L^2(\Omega )^3\) and \(\operatorname {div}\psi =0\) already say that \(\psi \in X(\Omega )\).

  • Boundary conditions and fluxes are the paper’s weak definitions by Green’s formulas (Definitions 2.2 and 2.4). The flux conditions are imposed for every admissible cutoff and lift, which are shown to exist (Lemma 2.6); the paper shows that the fluxes do not depend on these choices.

  • Not part of the statements: the additional sentences of Theorems 3.12 and 3.17 (linear dependence of \(\psi \) on \(u\) and the bound \(\lVert \psi \rVert _X\le C_\Omega \lVert u\rVert \)). For the tangential potential, linearity and continuity in \(L^2(\Omega )^3\) are proved in the library (Lemma 4.5).

  • Not formalized: the other results of the paper (regularity and compactness of Section 2, the harmonic kernels, Helmholtz decompositions and the full Dirichlet potential of Section 3, and the finite-element and Stokes results of Section 4). Their mathematical proofs are in blueprint/DetailedProofs.pdf.
