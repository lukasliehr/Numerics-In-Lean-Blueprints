6 The cuts

Lemma 6.1 The cuts are null
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3.3

Lean declarations

  • VP.GraphPatch.graph_null
  • VP.cuts_null
  • VP.measure_cutDomain_eq

Every graph patch is Lebesgue null. Hence the union of the cuts is null, and the measures of \(\Omega \) and \(\Omega ^{\circ }\) restricted from Lebesgue measure coincide.

Proof ▶

Fubini’s theorem in the coordinates of the patch: each vertical line meets the graph in one point. Each cut is covered by finitely many patches in which it is a graph.

Lemma 6.2 Unit-jump lifts
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3.4
  • Lemma 6.1

Lean declarations

  • VP.cutCutoff
  • VP.liftFn
  • VP.liftH1
  • VP.liftH1_unitJump
  • VP.liftQ
  • VP.hasWeakCurl_liftQ

For each cut \(\Sigma _j\) let \(\chi _j\) be a smooth cutoff equal to \(1\) near \(\overline{\Sigma _j}\) and supported in the union of its patches, and let \(M_j\) be the union of the minus sides. The lift \(\ell _j=\chi _j\mathbf1_{M_j}\) belongs to \(H^1(\Omega ^{\circ })\) and has unit jump across \(\Sigma _j\). Its gradient \(\mathbf1_{M_j}\nabla \chi _j\) is smooth on \(\Omega \), belongs to \(H^1(\Omega )^3\) and is curl free on \(\Omega \).

Proof ▶

Off the cut, \(\ell _j\) is locally \(\chi _j\) or \(0\), hence smooth; near \(\Sigma _j\), \(\chi _j=1\), so \(\ell _j\) is locally constant on each side. The gradient vanishes near the cuts and is a gradient elsewhere.

Lemma 6.3 Gluing across the cuts
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.cut_glue

If \(q\in H^1(\Omega ^{\circ })\) has unit jump across \(\Sigma _j\), there is \(r\in H^1(\Omega )\) with \(\nabla r=\nabla q-\nabla \ell _j\), the gradients of \(q\) and \(\ell _j\) being extended across the null cuts.

Proof ▶

\(q-\ell _j\) has no jump across any cut. In each patch ball it is the restriction of an \(H^1\) function by definition of the jump; away from the cuts it is \(H^1\) already. A partition of unity glues the local weak gradients.

Lemma 6.4 Gradients of unit-jump functions are curl free
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.3
  • Lemma 5.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.cutCurlFree_input

If \(q\in H^1(\Omega ^{\circ })\) has unit jump across a cut, its extended gradient is curl free on \(\Omega \).

Proof ▶

It is \(\nabla r+\nabla \ell _j\) with \(r\in H^1(\Omega )\) (Lemma 6.3); both terms are curl free (Lemmas 5.2 and 6.2).

Lemma 6.5 Cut fluxes of curls
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.3
  • Definition 3.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.cutFlux_input

If \(\psi \times n=0\) for a field \(\psi \) with \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\), and \(u\cdot n=0\), then \(C_j(u)=0\) for all \(j\).

Proof ▶

For \(q\) with unit jump across \(\Sigma _j\) write \(\nabla q=\nabla r+\nabla \ell _j\). Then \((u,\nabla r)=0\) since \(u\cdot n=0\), and \((u,\nabla \ell _j)=(\psi ,\operatorname {curl}\nabla \ell _j)=0\) by \(\psi \times n=0\) tested with \(\nabla \ell _j\in H^1(\Omega )^3\).
