Lemma 8.1 Zero tangential trace forces zero normal trace
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.4
  • Definition 2.3
  • Definition 2.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.ext0
  • VP.weakDiv_ext0_of_tangential
  • VP.orth_H1_of_weakDiv
  • VP.normalTrace_input

If \(\psi \times n=0\) for a field \(\psi \) with \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\), then \(u\cdot n=0\).

Proof ▶

Testing \(\psi \times n=0\) with gradients of smooth compactly supported functions on \(\mathbb {R}^3\) shows that the extension \(\bar u\) of \(u\) by zero is divergence free on \(\mathbb {R}^3\). For \(q\in H^1(\Omega )\), a smooth partition of unity subordinate to \(\Omega \) and to boundary graph patches localizes \(q\). In a patch, \(\bar u\) translated along the frame vanishes near the boundary graph, so it only meets a compactly supported cut-off of the localized function (Lemma 6.4); continuity of translation gives \(\int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla q=0\).

Lemma 8.2 Poincaré inequalities by segments
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • VP.poincare_star
  • VP.PoincareSet
  • VP.PoincareSet.of_star
  • VP.PoincareSet.union

Let \(S\subset \mathbb {R}^3\) be star shaped with respect to a set \(Z\) of positive finite measure, with \(\lvert w-x\rvert \le d\) for \(x\in S\), \(w\in Z\). Then every smooth \(\varphi \) admits \(c\in \mathbb {R}\) with

\[ \int _S\lvert \varphi -c\rvert ^2\le \frac{8d^2(\lvert S\rvert +\lvert Z\rvert )}{\lvert Z\rvert }\int _S\lvert \nabla \varphi \rvert ^2. \]

Sets carrying such an inequality glue along overlaps of positive measure.

Proof ▶

The fundamental theorem of calculus along the segments from \(x\in S\) to \(w\in Z\), Cauchy–Schwarz, and the affine changes of variables \(x\mapsto (1-t)x+tw\) and \(w\mapsto (1-t)x+tw\).

Lemma 8.3 Boundary charts and Lipschitz calculus
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2.3
  • Lemma 8.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.Chart
  • VP.exists_chart
  • VP.weakGrad_of_lipschitz
  • VP.lipschitz_cut

Every boundary point has arbitrarily small charts: a box \(V\) in frame coordinates in which \(\Omega \) lies strictly above a globally Lipschitz graph and the exterior strictly below it. The exterior part \(S=V\setminus \overline\Omega \) is star shaped with respect to its bottom slab, and the reflection across the graph is a Lipschitz, measure-preserving involution exchanging the two sides. A Lipschitz function on \(\mathbb {R}^3\) has its almost-everywhere gradient as weak gradient, and a Lipschitz function vanishing on the zero set of a continuous function \(\Phi \) stays Lipschitz after being cut off to \(\{ \Phi {\gt}0\} \).

Proof ▶

The charts shrink the patches of the Lipschitz boundary. The weak gradient follows from Rademacher’s theorem and integration by parts along lines; the cut-off statement from the intermediate value theorem.

Lemma 8.4 The reflection estimate
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.3
  • Lemma 6.4

Lean declarations

  • VP.chart_estimate

Let \(u\) be divergence free on \(\Omega \), \(C\) a chart with exterior part \(S\) and Lipschitz constant \(K\), \(\zeta \) a smooth cutoff equal to \(1\) on the half box and supported in the box with \(\lvert \nabla \zeta \rvert \le C_\zeta \), and \(F\) smooth with support in the half box. Then

\[ \Bigl\lvert \int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla F\Bigr\rvert \le \lVert u\rVert _{L^2(\Omega )^3}\bigl(C_\zeta \lVert F\rVert _{L^2(S)}+(3+2K)\lVert \nabla F\rVert _{L^2(S)}\bigr). \]
Proof ▶

With \(R\) the reflection, \(D=F-\zeta \, F\circ R\) is Lipschitz and vanishes on the graph. Cut off to the side of \(\Omega \) and translated upward it becomes compactly supported in \(\Omega \), hence orthogonal to \(u\) through its gradient; continuity of translation gives \(\int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla F=\int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla (\zeta \, F\circ R)\), which is bounded by the norms of \(F\) and \(\nabla F\) on \(S\) because \(R\) exchanges the two sides and preserves measure.

Theorem 8.5 The key estimate
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.4
  • Lemma 8.2
  • Definition 2.4

Lean declarations

  • VP.ell
  • VP.key_estimate

Let \(u\in L^2(\Omega )^3\) be divergence free with \(F_i(u)=0\) for all \(i\). There are a bounded open set \(\Theta \) disjoint from \(\Omega \) and \(K{\lt}\infty \) with \(\lvert \int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla \varphi \rvert \le K\lVert \nabla \varphi \rVert _{L^2(\Theta )}\) for all smooth compactly supported \(\varphi \) on \(\mathbb {R}^3\).

Proof ▶

Choose charts small enough to meet a single boundary component, and a partition of unity subordinate to \(\Omega \) and finitely many charts. The interior piece of \(\int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla \varphi \) vanishes; each chart piece is controlled by Lemma 8.4; on each boundary component a constant can be subtracted from \(\varphi \) because the fluxes vanish. The Poincaré inequalities of the exterior chart pieces chain along the connected boundary components (Lemma 8.2).

Theorem 8.6 Solenoidal extension
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 8.5

Lean declarations

  • VP.exists_extension

A divergence-free \(u\in L^2(\Omega )^3\) with \(F_i(u)=0\) for all \(i\) is the restriction to \(\Omega \) of a compactly supported \(U\in L^2(\mathbb {R}^3)^3\) that is weakly divergence free on \(\mathbb {R}^3\).

Proof ▶

By Theorem 8.5, \(\nabla \varphi |_\Theta \mapsto \int _\Omega u\cdot \nabla \varphi \) is a bounded functional on gradients in \(L^2(\Theta )^3\). Extend it by Hahn–Banach and let \(w\) be its Riesz representative; then \(U=u\) on \(\Omega \), \(U=-w\) on \(\Theta \) and \(U=0\) elsewhere is divergence free on \(\mathbb {R}^3\).

Lemma 8.7 An explicit curl potential
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • VP.potOp
  • VP.weakCurl_potOp

Let \(v\in L^2(\mathbb {R}^3)^3\) vanish outside the ball of radius \(\rho \) and be weakly divergence free on \(\mathbb {R}^3\). If \(\rho +\rho '\le R\), then

\[ (Av)(x)=\Bigl(0,\ \int _0^Rv_3(x-te_1)\, \mathrm{d}t,\ -\int _0^Rv_2(x-te_1)\, \mathrm{d}t\Bigr) \]

belongs to \(L^2(\mathbb {R}^3)^3\) and has weak curl \(v\) on the ball of radius \(\rho '\).

Proof ▶

Transpose the line integrals (Fubini and translation invariance), so that the test field meets the adjoint line integrals; differentiate these under the integral sign. The fundamental theorem of calculus along \(e_1\) produces \(v\), because \(v(x-Re_1)=0\) for \(\lvert x\rvert {\lt}\rho '\), and the remaining gradient term vanishes because \(\operatorname {div}v=0\).

Theorem 8.9 Uniqueness of the normal potential
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 8.6
  • Lemma 8.7
  • Lemma 6.1
  • Lemma 6.2

Lean declarations

  • VP.weakCurl_ext0_of_tangential

If \(\psi \in L^2(\Omega )^3\) is divergence free, \(\psi \times n=0\) for \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =0\), and \(F_i(\psi )=0\) for all \(i\), then \(\psi =0\).

Proof ▶

Let \(U\) be the solenoidal extension of \(\psi \) (Theorem 8.6) and \(A=AU\) its potential (Lemma 8.7). The extension \(\bar\psi \) of \(\psi \) by zero is curl free on \(\mathbb {R}^3\) because \(\psi \times n=0\). Hence \(\lVert \psi \rVert ^2=\int U\cdot \bar\psi =\lim _{t\to 0}\int U\cdot (\rho _t*\bar\psi ) =\lim _{t\to 0}\int A\cdot \operatorname {curl}(\rho _t*\bar\psi )=0\).
