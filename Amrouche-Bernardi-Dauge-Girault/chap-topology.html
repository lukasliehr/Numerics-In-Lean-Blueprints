8 Topology of the cut domain

Lemma 8.1 Global primitives
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 2.8
  • Theorem 2.9

Lean declarations

  • VP.ClosedField
  • VP.exists_global_primitive

A smooth curl-free field on a simply connected open set \(U\subset \mathbb {R}^3\) is the gradient of a smooth function on \(U\).

Proof ▶

Local primitives on balls exist by the Poincaré lemma for convex sets. Pairs of a point and a value, topologized by the graphs of local primitives, form a covering space of \(U\); since \(U\) is simply connected, the identity of \(U\) lifts to it, which is a global primitive.

Lemma 8.2 Primitives jump by constants across the cuts
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.1
  • Definition 3.3

Lean declarations

  • VP.exists_cut_constant

Let \(h\) be a smooth curl-free field on \(\Omega \) and \(P\) a primitive of \(h\) on \(\Omega ^{\circ }\). Along each cut \(\Sigma _j\) the jump of \(P\) across \(\Sigma _j\) is a single constant.

Proof ▶

Near a point of a cut, small boxes in the frame of the cut patch have path-connected sides. On each side \(P\) differs from a local primitive of \(h\) on the box by a constant, so the jump is locally constant along the cut, and the cut is connected.

Theorem 8.3 Orthogonality to harmonic fields
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.2
  • Definition 3.4

Lean declarations

  • VP.harm_input

If \(h\in L^2(\Omega )^3\) is curl free and divergence free, and \(v\in L^2(\Omega )^3\) satisfies \(v\cdot n=0\) and \(C_j(v)=0\) for all \(j\), then \((v,h)_{L^2(\Omega )^3}=0\).

Proof ▶

By Theorem 7.3, \(h\) is smooth and curl free on \(\Omega \). On the simply connected cut domain it has a primitive \(P\), which jumps by constants \(c_j\) across the cuts (Lemma 8.2). Then \(p_0=P-\sum _jc_j\ell _j\) is locally smooth on \(\Omega \) with \(\nabla p_0=h-\sum _jc_j\nabla \ell _j\). The bounded truncations \(n\arctan (p_0/n)\) lie in \(H^1(\Omega )\), so \(v\cdot n=0\) and dominated convergence give \((v,\nabla p_0)=0\), while \(C_j(v)=0\) gives \((v,\nabla \ell _j)=0\).
