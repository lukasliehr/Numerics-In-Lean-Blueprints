1 Overview

This blueprint describes a Lean 4 formalization of the two theorems on canonical vector potentials of

C. Amrouche, C. Bernardi, M. Dauge, V. Girault, Vector potentials in three-dimensional non-smooth domains, Math. Methods Appl. Sci. 21 (1998), no. 9, 823–864.

Let \(\Omega \subset \mathbb {R}^3\) be a bounded connected Lipschitz domain whose boundary has the connected components \(\Gamma _0,\dots ,\Gamma _I\) (\(\Gamma _0\) the outer one), and let \(\Sigma _1,\dots ,\Sigma _J\) be an admissible system of cuts, so that \(\Omega ^{\circ }=\Omega \setminus (\Sigma _1\cup \dots \cup \Sigma _J)\) is simply connected. For \(u\in L^2(\Omega )^3\):

  • Theorem 3.12. \(\operatorname {div}u=0\) and \(u\) has zero flux through every \(\Gamma _i\) if and only if there is exactly one \(\psi \) with \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\), \(\operatorname {div}\psi =0\), \(\psi \cdot n=0\) on \(\partial \Omega \), and zero flux through every cut \(\Sigma _j\) (the tangential vector potential).

  • Theorem 3.17. \(\operatorname {div}u=0\), \(u\cdot n=0\) on \(\partial \Omega \) and \(u\) has zero flux through every cut if and only if there is exactly one \(\psi \) with \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\), \(\operatorname {div}\psi =0\), \(\psi \times n=0\) on \(\partial \Omega \), and zero flux through every \(\Gamma _i\) (the normal vector potential).

The Lean files.

  • Showcase.lean imports only Mathlib. It defines the setting and states both theorems in the notation of the paper (\(\operatorname {div}u=0\), \(\operatorname {curl}\psi =u\), \(\psi \cdot n=0\), \(\psi \times n=0\), \(F_i(u)=0\), \(C_j(\psi )=0\)), with sorry.

  • Showcase_WithProofs.lean proves the same theorems from the library LeanCode.

  • comparator.json configures lake comparator. It checks that the proofs establish exactly the statements of Showcase.lean and use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

  • The library LeanCode contains about 8 400 lines of proofs, organized as in the chapters below. LeanCode/Definitions.lean repeats the definitions of Showcase.lean verbatim.

  • blueprint/DetailedProofs.pdf contains detailed mathematical proofs of all principal results of the paper. Its arguments for the two theorems formalized here partly differ from the route of the Lean proof described below.

The route.

The proof has two layers. An abstract Hilbert-space argument (Chapter 5) derives both theorems from seven analytic facts, collected in Definition 5.1. The other chapters prove these facts for every admissible geometric datum: the cuts are null and carry unit-jump lifts (Chapter 6); curl-free, divergence-free fields are smooth (Chapter 7) and, on the simply connected cut domain, gradients (Chapter 8); and divergence-free fields with zero boundary fluxes extend to solenoidal fields on \(\mathbb {R}^3\), which are curls of an explicit line-integral potential (Chapter 9).
