Proposition 7.1 DG coercivity, Prop. 3.2
✓

Uses

Lean declarations

  • BHS.exists_dgCoercivity

There is \(\varepsilon _0\) such that for \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\), \(B^{\mathrm{DG}}(\mu ,\mu )\ge c\lVert \mu \rVert ^2_{\mathrm{DG}}\) on \(V_h\), with \(c\) independent of the mesh, \(\kappa \), \(a\) and \(\varepsilon \).

Proof ▶

For polynomial fields, \(B^{\mathrm{DG}}(p,p)=\kappa \lvert p\rvert ^2_{H^1}+\frac12\lVert p\rVert ^2_C+\varepsilon J_0(p)-(1-s)\kappa \sum _e\int _e \nabla p^-\cdot [\! [p]\! ]\), with \(J_0(p)=\sum _e\frac{\kappa }{h_{\perp }}\lVert [\! [p]\! ]\rVert ^2_{L^2(e)}\). Young’s inequality, the gradient trace inequality and \(h_{\perp }\lesssim h_T\) bound the last term by \(\frac12\kappa \lvert p\rvert ^2_{H^1}+CJ_0(p)\), which is absorbed for \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\). The constant \(1/(2(1+K))\) is uniform in \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\).

There are \(\varepsilon _0,\tau _{\max }{\gt}0\) such that for \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\) and \(0\le \tau \le \tau _{\max }\), \(B^{\mathrm{SDG}}(\mu ,\mu )\ge c\lVert \mu \rVert ^2_{\mathrm{SDG}}\) on \(V_h\).

  • BHS.exists_dgInfSup

There is \(\varepsilon _0\) such that for all \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\) and \(\tau {\gt}0\) there is \(\beta {\gt}0\) with: for every \(\nu \in V_h\) some \(\mu \in V_h\) satisfies \(\lVert \mu \rVert _{\mathrm{SDG}}\le \lVert \nu \rVert _{\mathrm{SDG}}\) and \(B^{\mathrm{DG}}(\nu ,\mu )\ge \beta \lVert \nu \rVert ^2_{\mathrm{SDG}}\).

Proof ▶

Lemma 7.5 Local inf-sup, Lemma 4.4

  • BHS.exists_localInfSup

There is \(\varepsilon _0\) such that for \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\), \(\tau {\gt}0\) and every bound \(\delta _{\max }\ge 0\) there is \(\beta _L{\gt}0\) such that the local forms \(b_T\) satisfy the normalized inf-sup condition in \(\lVert \cdot \rVert _{\mathrm{SDG}(T)}\) for all \(\delta \in [0,\delta _{\max }]\).

Proof ▶

As in Theorem 3.5, the witness is \(\nu +\gamma \tau _T(a\cdot \nabla \nu )\), normalized. It uses local coercivity of \(b_T\) for \(\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon _0\), a lower bound in the streamline direction, and the inherited face thickness, which is comparable to \(h_T\) by shape regularity.

Lemma 7.6 Local Poincaré inequality, Lemma 4.5
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 5.7
  • Lemma 5.4

Lean declarations

  • BHS.exists_localPoincare

\(\tau _T^{-1}\lVert \nu \rVert ^2_{L^2(T)}\le C\lVert \nu \rVert ^2_{\mathrm{SDG}(T)}\) (without the Hessian term) for every \(H^1\) jet \(\nu \) with traces.

Proof ▶

Instead of the paper’s Lipschitz function \(\eta \), apply the divergence theorem to \((a\cdot (x-v_0))\, w^2\), where \(v_0\) is a vertex; this gives \(\lvert a\rvert ^2\lVert w\rVert ^2_T\le 2\lvert a\rvert h\sum _j\lVert \lvert a\cdot n_j\rvert ^{1/2}w\rVert ^2_{F_j}+4h^2\lVert a\cdot \nabla w\rVert ^2_T\). The radial identity gives the diffusive bound \(d^2\lVert w\rVert ^2_T\le 2dh\lVert w\rVert ^2_{F_j}+4h^2\lvert w\rvert ^2_{H^1}\). Both hold for \(C^1\) functions and pass to Sobolev jets through their smooth approximants.

For every exact solution \(\varphi \) (with \(k\ge 1\)), \(B^{\mathrm{DG}}(\varphi ,\mu )=L^{\mathrm{DG}}(\mu )\) and \(B^{\mathrm{SDG}}(\varphi ,\mu )=L^{\mathrm{SDG}}(\mu )\) for all \(\mu \in V_h\).

Theorem 7.8 Unique solvability
✓
#

Uses

  • Proposition 7.1
  • Proposition 7.2
  • Lemma 7.5

Lean declarations

  • BHS.existsUnique_dgSolution
  • BHS.existsUnique_sdgSolution
  • BHS.existsUnique_smdgSolution
  • BHS.existsUnique_transfer

DG, SDG, SMDG and the local transfer problems have unique solutions.

Proof ▶

The discrete norms are definite on \(V_h\). A field with vanishing energy is piecewise constant with vanishing jumps (including the boundary). The divergence theorem for \(x\mapsto \langle e_0,x\rangle \) on every cell then gives \(\sum _Tc_T^2\lvert T\rvert =0\). Coercivity and the inf-sup conditions give injectivity, and finite dimension gives surjectivity. SMDG is posed on the linear space \(\mathfrak {T}_h(\overline{V}_h,0)\), shifted by a particular transfer.
