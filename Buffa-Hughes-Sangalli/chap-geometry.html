5 Geometry of simplicial meshes

Lemma 5.1 Heights and shape regularity
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2.1
  • Definition 3.1

Lean declarations

  • BHS.two_mul_le_height
  • BHS.cellSize_le_height
  • BHS.cellVolume_eq_height_mul_facetArea

If a cell contains a ball of radius \(r\) then every height \(H_j\ge 2r\); hence \(h_T\le \frac\rho 2H_j\). Moreover \(\lvert T\rvert =\frac1d H_j\lvert F_j\rvert \).

Proof ▶

The inscribed ball lies between the facet hyperplane and the parallel hyperplane through the opposite vertex. The volume formula is the cone formula.

Lemma 5.2 Normals and face combinatorics
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3.1

Lean declarations

  • BHS.normals_eq_outwardNormal
  • BHS.sum_cellFacets_eq_sum_faces

The mesh normals are the outward normals of the simplices. Sums over the facets of all cells can be regrouped into sums over faces (one or two sides each).

Proof ▶

Admissibility gives a bijection between cell facets and face sides.

Lemma 5.3 Interior points of faces
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 5.2

Lean declarations

  • BHS.exists_halfBall_subset
  • BHS.mem_interior_union_of_relint

Near a relative interior point of a facet, the cell contains a half ball. A point in the relative interior of an interior face is an interior point of the union of its two cells.

Proof ▶

Barycentric coordinates and the opposite normals of the two sides.
