Assume SDG stability, the DG and local inf-sup conditions, the local Poincaré inequality, consistency, and an interpolation premise with constant \(C_I\). Then the SDG, DG and SMDG errors in \(\lVert \cdot \rVert _{\mathrm{SDG}}\), and the transfer approximation error in \(|\! |\! |\cdot |\! |\! |_{\mathrm{SDG}}\), are bounded by \(C\) times the error indicator. Here \(C\) depends only on \(d,\rho ,\varepsilon \) and the given constants.

Proof ▶

Galerkin orthogonality, together with coercivity (SDG, SMDG) or the inf-sup witness (DG), and continuity. For the transfer, consistency of the local problems gives an exact identity for \(b_T(c-\nu ,\mu )\); the local inf-sup condition and Lemma 8.1 conclude.
