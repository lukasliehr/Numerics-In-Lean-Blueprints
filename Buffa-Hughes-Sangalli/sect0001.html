1 Overview

This blueprint describes a complete Lean 4 formalization of the main results of

A. Buffa, T. J. R. Hughes, G. Sangalli, Analysis of a multiscale discontinuous Galerkin method for convection-diffusion problems, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 44 (2006), no. 4, 1420–1440.

The paper studies the convection-diffusion problem

\[ -\kappa \Delta \varphi + a\cdot \nabla \varphi = f \text{ in } \Omega ,\qquad \varphi = g \text{ on } \partial \Omega , \]

discretized by an interior-penalty discontinuous Galerkin (DG) method with upwind fluxes, its streamline-diffusion stabilization (SDG), and the multiscale variant (MDG/SMDG). In the multiscale variant, the discontinuous trial and test functions are obtained from a continuous coarse field by solving local DG problems on the elements. The main results are coercivity and continuity of the DG and SDG forms (Propositions 3.2, 3.3), an inf-sup condition for the DG form in the stronger SDG norm (Theorem 3.5), and stability of the local problems (Lemmas 4.4, 4.5). They also include optimal a-priori error estimates

\[ \lVert \varphi -\varphi _h\rVert _{\mathrm{SDG}} \lesssim \Bigl(\sum _{T\in \mathcal{T}_h}\bigl(\lvert a\rvert \, h_T^{2k+1}+\kappa \, h_T^{2k}\bigr) \lvert \varphi \rvert _{H^{k+1}(T)}^2\Bigr)^{1/2} \]

for DG, SDG and SMDG, and the corresponding approximation property of the multiscale trial spaces (Proposition 3.4, Corollary 3.7, Theorems 4.3 and 4.6). All constants are independent of the mesh, the domain, \(\kappa \) and \(a\).

The Lean files.

  • Showcase.lean imports only Mathlib. It defines the setting and states each numbered result of the paper as its own theorem, in the paper’s notation and with the paper’s quantifiers, with sorry.

  • Showcase_WithProofs.lean proves the same theorems from the library LeanCode.

  • comparator.json configures lake comparator. It checks that the proofs establish exactly the statements of Showcase.lean and use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

  • The library LeanCode contains about 18 000 lines of proofs, organized as in the chapters below. Chapter 10 and the file Audit/paper_correspondence.md compare the formal statements with the paper.

Scope.

The setting is the paper’s, specialized to: dimension \(d\in \{ 2,3\} \); every polynomial degree \(k\ge 1\); conforming shape-regular affine simplicial meshes of a bounded connected Lipschitz domain; and constant coefficients \(\kappa {\gt}0\), \(a\in \mathbb {R}^d\) (including \(a=0\)), with no positive lower bound on \(\kappa \).
