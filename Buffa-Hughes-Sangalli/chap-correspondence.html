10 Correspondence with the paper

The detailed comparison is in Audit/paper_correspondence.md. In summary:

  • All numbered results of Sections 3 and 4 are stated: Propositions 3.2–3.4, Theorem 3.5, Corollary 3.7, Theorem 4.3, Lemmas 4.4 and 4.5, and Theorem 4.6. They have the paper’s uniformity: constants independent of the mesh, the domain, \(\kappa \) and \(a\). The quantifier order is the paper’s.

  • Scope: simplicial meshes, constant coefficients, and \(\kappa {\gt}0\).

  • Repairs of printed formulas: the \(\kappa \)-weight of the jump term in (3.7) is linear; the sign of the \(s\)-term follows (3.2); continuity (3.17) holds on broken \(H^2\); \(h_{\perp }\) in (4.2) is the global face thickness; Lemma 4.5 is stated on \(H^1\) without the Hessian term.

  • Not formalized: Section 5 (numerics), Remark 3.6, and the open problem of Remark 4.7.
