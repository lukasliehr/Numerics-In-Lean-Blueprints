Numerics in Lean

Lean 4 and Mathlib formalizations of three research papers in numerical analysis and the analysis of partial differential equations. Each blueprint has one node per definition and result, linked to the Lean declarations that prove it, and an interactive dependency graph.

Analysis of a multiscale discontinuous Galerkin method for convection-diffusion problems

A. Buffa, T. J. R. Hughes, G. Sangalli. SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 44 (2006), 1420–1440. doi:10.1137/050640382

DG, SDG and multiscale DG methods: coercivity, inf-sup conditions, local stability and error estimates, all numbered results of Sections 3 and 4.

Unified analysis of discontinuous Galerkin methods for elliptic problems

D. N. Arnold, F. Brezzi, B. Cockburn, L. D. Marini. SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 39 (2002), 1749–1779. doi:10.1137/S0036142901384162

Flux and primal formulations, consistency, stability and error estimates for all nine methods of the paper, and the H² regularity of the Dirichlet problem on convex polygons.

Vector potentials in three-dimensional non-smooth domains

C. Amrouche, C. Bernardi, M. Dauge, V. Girault. Math. Methods Appl. Sci. 21 (1998), 823–864. doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-1476(199806)21:9<823::AID-MMA976>3.0.CO;2-B

Existence and uniqueness of tangential and normal vector potentials on bounded Lipschitz domains with cuts (Theorems 3.12 and 3.17).
