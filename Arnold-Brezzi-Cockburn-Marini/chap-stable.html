Lemma 8.1 Liftings
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3.6
  • Lemma 5.7

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.isLiftR_liftR
  • ABCM.isLiftL_liftL
  • ABCM.exists_liftR_upper
  • ABCM.exists_liftL_upper
  • ABCM.exists_liftR_lower

The liftings \(r_e(\varphi )\) and \(l_e(q)\) exist and are unique. Uniformly over admissible meshes: \(h_e\lVert r_e(\varphi )\rVert _\Omega ^2\le C\lVert \varphi \rVert _e^2\), \(h_e\lVert l_e(q)\rVert _\Omega ^2\le C\lVert q\rVert _e^2\), and \(c\lVert [\! [v_h]\! ]\rVert _e^2\le h_e\lVert r_e([\! [v_h]\! ])\rVert _\Omega ^2\) for \(v_h\in V_h\).

Proof ▶

Existence by Riesz representation in the finite-dimensional space \(\Sigma _h\); the upper bounds by Cauchy–Schwarz on the edge and the discrete trace inequality; the lower bound by testing with a polynomial extension of the jump from the edge into an adjacent triangle.

Lemma 8.2 Bounds for the pieces of the forms
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 5.4
  • Lemma 5.7
  • Definition 3.8

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.exists_avgGrad_trace
  • ABCM.exists_jumpGrad_trace
  • ABCM.exists_jump_trace
  • ABCM.exists_fluxC_bound

For broken \(H^2\) fields, uniformly over admissible meshes, \(\sum _eh_e\lVert \{ \nabla w\} \rVert _e^2\) and \(\sum _eh_e\lVert [\! [\nabla w]\! ]\rVert _e^2\) are bounded by \(C(G_h(w)^2+H_h(w)^2)\), and \(\sum _eh_e^{-1}\lVert [\! [w]\! ]\rVert _e^2\le C\sum _K(h_K^{-2}\lVert w\rVert _K^2+ \lvert w\rvert _{H^1(K)}^2)\). Consequently \(\lvert C(w,v)\rvert \le CN_h(w)N_h(v)\), and similarly for \(g\), \(A_j\) and \(D_\beta \).

Proof ▶

The trace inequality of Lemma 5.4 applied to the first derivatives, and Cauchy–Schwarz.

Lemma 8.4 Coercivity
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.2
  • Lemma 8.1
  • Lemma 5.7

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.ip_coercive_core
  • ABCM.ldg_coercive_core
  • ABCM.exists_std_coercive

There is \(\eta _*(p,\kappa ){\gt}0\) such that each of the four methods, under its threshold (IP: \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}\eta _*\); Bassi et al.: \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}3\); Brezzi et al. and LDG: \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}0\)), satisfies \(C_sN_h(v_h)^2\le B(v_h,v_h)\) on \(V_h\) with \(C_s{\gt}0\) uniform over admissible meshes and coefficients.

Proof ▶

Since \((R(v),\nabla _hv)=-C(v,v)\) by the defining identity of the liftings: for IP, \(C(v,v)\) is absorbed by the jump penalty when \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}\eta _*\) (inverse and discrete trace inequalities); for Bassi et al. it is absorbed by \(A_r\) when \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}3\), because every triangle has three edges; for Brezzi et al. the form equals \(\lVert \nabla _hv+R(v)\rVert ^2+A_r(v,v)\), and LDG contains the analogous square with \(A_\beta \), so every \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}0\) suffices. The penalty controls \(J_h\) by the lower bound of Lemma 8.1, and \(H_h\) is controlled by \(G_h\) on \(V_h\) by the inverse inequality.

Lemma 8.5 Unique solvability
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 2.10
  • Definition 3.9
  • Definition 3.8

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.existsUnique_of_injective
  • ABCM.eq_zero_of_normN_poly
  • ABCM.existsUnique_discrete

A bilinear form on a finite-dimensional space whose trial map is injective is uniquely solvable. \(N_h\) is a norm on \(V_h\); hence a form that is bilinear and coercive in \(N_h\) on \(V_h\) has exactly one discrete solution for every \(f\in L^2(\Omega )\).

Proof ▶

If \(N_h(v_h)=0\), then \(v_h\) is piecewise constant with zero jumps, and the divergence argument \(\sum _Kc_K^2\lvert K\rvert =0\) forces \(v_h=0\).

Lemma 8.6 Green’s formula for broken fields
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 5.5
  • Lemma 5.6

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.green_poly

For an exact solution \(u\) with \(-\Delta u=f\) on every cell and every \(v_h\in V_h\): \(g(u,v_h)-C(u,v_h)=(f,v_h)_\Omega \).

Proof ▶

Green’s formula on each cell; the edge terms combine into \(C(u,v_h)\) because the traces of \(\nabla u\) agree across interior edges.

Lemma 8.7 Consistency of the four stable methods
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.6
  • Lemma 5.6
  • Lemma 8.1

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.std_consistency_core

If \(u\in H^2(\Omega )\cap H^1_0(\Omega )\) with \(-\Delta u=f\) is realized by \(U\), then \(B(U,v)=B(v,U)=(f,v)_\Omega \) for all \(v\in W_h\) and each of the four stable methods.

Proof ▶

The jumps of \(U\) vanish, so all penalty and lifting terms containing \([\! [U]\! ]\) vanish, and the remaining terms reduce to Lemma 8.6; symmetry gives adjoint consistency.

Lemma 8.8 The abstract energy estimate
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 3.8
  • Definition 3.9

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.energy_error

If \(B\) is linear in its first argument, bounded by \(C_bN_hN_h\), coercive with constant \(C_s\) on \(V_h\) and consistent at \(U\), then every discrete solution satisfies \(N_h(U-u_h)\le 2(1+C_b/C_s)N_h(U-v_h)\) for every \(v_h\in V_h\).

Proof ▶

\(C_sN_h(v_h-u_h)^2\le B(v_h-u_h,v_h-u_h)=B(v_h-U,v_h-u_h)\le C_bN_h(U-v_h)N_h(v_h-u_h)\) and the triangle inequality.

Lemma 8.9 The duality argument
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.8
  • Definition 3.8

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.l2_duality

Let \(B\) be bilinear, bounded on broken \(H^2\) fields, consistent at \(U\) and adjoint consistent on \(W_h\); assume \(H^2\) regularity with constant \(C_{reg}\) and an \(O(h)\) interpolant for \(H^2\) jets. Then \(\lVert U-u_h\rVert _{L^2}\le C_bC_IC_{reg}\, h\, N_h(U-u_h)\).

Proof ▶

Aubin–Nitsche: test the adjoint problem with the error, subtract the interpolant of the dual solution using Galerkin orthogonality, and bound with \(C_b\).
