For each method and each triangle, \(-(u_h,\operatorname {div}\tau )_K+\langle \hat u,\tau \cdot n_K\rangle _{\partial K}=(\sigma _h^*,\tau )_K\), where \(\sigma _h^*\in \Sigma _h\) is the eliminated auxiliary field; and \((\sigma _h^*,\nabla w)-\sum _K\langle \hat\sigma \cdot n_K,w\rangle _{\partial K}=B_h(u_h,w)\) for all \(w\in V_h\).

Proof ▶

Integration by parts on each triangle and the defining identities of the liftings, which turn the edge terms of \(\hat u\) into \(R\), \(L_\beta \) or \(A_\beta \); the edge terms of \(\hat\sigma \) give \(C\), the penalties and the lifting products.
