10 NIPG, pure penalties and superpenalties

Lemma 10.1 Stability of the penalty methods
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.2
  • Lemma 8.1

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.exists_penalty_bound
  • ABCM.exists_penalty_coercive
  • ABCM.super_coercive

NIPG and the pure penalty forms are bounded on \(W_h\) and coercive on \(V_h\) in \(N_h\), for every \(\eta _{\min }{\gt}0\); the superpenalized forms are coercive in their superpenalty norms.

Proof ▶

For NIPG the terms \(\mp C\) cancel in \(B(v,v)\); the pure penalties contain no \(C\). Boundedness as in Lemma 8.3.

Lemma 10.2 Broken Poincaré inequality
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 4.1
  • Lemma 8.6
  • Lemma 8.2

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.exists_broken_poincare

On a convex polygon there is \(C_P\) with \(\lVert v\rVert _{L^2(\Omega )}\le C_PN_h(v)\) for all \(v\in W_h\), uniformly over admissible meshes.

Proof ▶

Duality: solve \(-\Delta \psi =v\) with \(H^2\) regularity and integrate by parts cellwise against \(v\).

Lemma 10.4 Error estimates with consistency defects
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.8
  • Lemma 8.9
  • Lemma 10.3

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.super_energy
  • ABCM.super_l2
  • ABCM.super_assembly

For a coercive bounded form whose primal defect is \((1-\varepsilon )C(u,\cdot )\), bounded by \(\Gamma \) in the method’s norm, every discrete solution satisfies \(N(U-u_h)\le 2\bigl((1+C_b/C_s)N(U-v_h)+\lvert 1-\varepsilon \rvert \Gamma /C_s\bigr)\); a duality version gives the \(L^2\) estimate when the adjoint defect is \((1+\varepsilon )C(\psi ,\cdot )\). For the three superpenalized methods these give the \(h^p\) and \(h^{p+1}\) estimates.

Proof ▶

As in Lemmas 8.8 and 8.9, carrying the defect; by Lemma 10.3 the defect is of order \(h^p\).
