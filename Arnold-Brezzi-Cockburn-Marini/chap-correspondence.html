12 Correspondence with the paper

  • Formalized: the principal quantitative results for all nine methods of Table 6.1 of the paper and the three superpenalized methods: the flux–primal equivalence (Section 3.2), consistency and adjoint consistency (Section 3.3), boundedness and stability (Section 4), the error estimates of Section 5.1 for the four stable methods, NIPG, the pure penalties and the superpenalties (Section 5.2), Baumann–Oden (Section 5.3.1) and the original Bassi–Rebay method (Section 5.3.2).

  • Setting: \(\Omega \) is a bounded open convex polygon; meshes are conforming shape-regular triangulations, without quasi-uniformity. The \(H^2\) regularity of the Dirichlet problem, used by the paper’s duality arguments, is proved (Theorem 3.1).

  • Norms: the DG norm \(N_h\) includes the scaled broken \(H^2\) term, which makes it uniformly equivalent to the lifting-based norm (4.2) of the paper on \(V_h\).

  • Qualifications made explicit: Baumann–Oden is analysed for \(p\ge 2\), with a counterexample for \(p=1\); for Bassi–Rebay the solution is unique only modulo \(Z_h\), the estimates concern \(\mathcal{D}_hu_h\) and \(P_{p-1}u_h\), and the full \(L^2\) error is shown to be uncontrolled.

  • Not formalized: the historical discussion, the qualitative comparisons and the open questions of the paper.
