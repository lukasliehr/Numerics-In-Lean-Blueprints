Lemma 12.1 The Bassi–Rebay form is a square
✓

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.discreteGrad_green

On \(V_h\times V_h\), \(B_{BR}(w,v)=(\mathcal{D}_hw,\mathcal{D}_hv)_\Omega \). For \(v\in V_h\) and \(\tau \in \Sigma _h\): \((\mathcal{D}_hv,\tau )=-(v,\operatorname {div}_h\tau )+\sum _{e\text{ interior}}\langle \{ v\} ,[\! [\tau ]\! ]\rangle _e\).

Lemma 12.2 Local Raviart–Thomas constructions
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 2.11
  • Lemma 5.1
  • Lemma 5.7

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.exists_ref_bubble
  • ABCM.BR.exists_local_lift

On every triangle there are polynomial vector fields of degree at most \(p\) with prescribed compatible normal traces on the edges and prescribed divergence of degree at most \(p-1\), with bounds uniform under shape regularity.

Proof ▶

Lowest-order Raviart–Thomas fields for the normal traces, edge bubbles \(\lambda _a\lambda _b\sigma (P_b-P_a)\) for the divergence, transported from a reference triangle.

Lemma 12.3 The kernel
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 12.1
  • Lemma 12.2

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.brKernel_iff

For \(v_h\in V_h\): \(\mathcal{D}_hv_h=0\) if and only if \(P_{p-1}v_h=0\) on every triangle and \(\{ v_h\} =0\) on every interior edge.

Proof ▶

Test \((\mathcal{D}_hv_h,\tau )=0\) (Lemma 12.1) with fields supported on one triangle: edge bubbles force \(P_{p-1}v_h=0\), and fields with prescribed normal traces force \(\{ v_h\} =0\).

Lemma 12.4 Solvability
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 12.1

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.brSolvable_iff
  • ABCM.BR.isDiscreteSolution_iff_kernel
  • ABCM.BR.discreteGrad_unique

The problem is solvable if and only if \(f\perp Z_h\); the solutions form one coset of \(Z_h\), and \(\mathcal{D}_hu_h\) and \(P_{p-1}u_h\) do not depend on the solution.

Proof ▶

A finite-dimensional Fredholm alternative for the symmetric form \((\mathcal{D}_h\cdot ,\mathcal{D}_h\cdot )\), whose kernel is \(Z_h\); \(P_{p-1}\) vanishes on \(Z_h\) by Lemma 12.3.

Lemma 12.5 The conservative interpolant of the flux
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 12.2
  • Lemma 12.1
  • Lemma 5.4

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.exists_consInterp

Let \(u\in H^2(\Omega )\) solve \(-\Delta u=f\) with \(f\) elementwise polynomial of degree at most \(p-1\). For every \(w\in V_h\) there is \(\sigma \in \Sigma _h\) with \((\mathcal{D}_hv_h,\sigma )=(f,v_h)\) for all \(v_h\in V_h\) and \(\lVert \nabla u-\sigma \rVert ^2\le C\bigl(G_h(u-w)^2+H_h(u-w)^2\bigr)\).

Proof ▶

Build \(\sigma \) triangle by triangle from \(\nabla w\) with Lemma 12.2, with single-valued normal traces and \(\operatorname {div}\sigma =-f\).

Lemma 12.7 The full \(L^2\) error is not controlled
✓
#

Uses

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.BR.oneTri_br_example

For every \(p\ge 1\), on the one-triangle mesh, \(z=x_0^p-P_{p-1}(x_0^p)\) is a nonzero element of \(Z_h\); every \(tz\) solves the problem with \(f=0\), and \((z,z)\ne 0\), so \(f=z\) has no solution.

Proof ▶

There are no interior edges, so by Lemma 12.3 the kernel consists of the polynomials of degree at most \(p\) orthogonal to \(\mathbb P_{p-1}\).
