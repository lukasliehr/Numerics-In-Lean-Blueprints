6 Interpolation

Lemma 6.1 Lagrange interpolation
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 4.1

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.lagInterp
  • ABCM.evalPoly_lagInterp
  • ABCM.coarseField_lagInterp

On a simplex with barycentric coordinates \(b_i\) and degree \(k\ge 1\), the explicit Lagrange basis on the principal lattice interpolates at the lattice nodes; the interpolant on a facet depends only on the values on that facet, so cellwise interpolants of a continuous function agree on common edges.

Proof ▶

The basis functions are products of affine factors in the barycentric coordinates.

Lemma 6.2 Continuous representatives
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 4.10
  • Lemma 4.4
  • Lemma 4.6

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.exists_continuousRep
  • ABCM.continuousRep_glue

Under the scaled sup bound, a jet with \(H^2\) traces on a cell has a continuous representative \(w\) on the closed cell, equal to the traces on the facets, with \(\lvert T\rvert (w-q)(x)^2\le C\, E_{H^2}(J-q)\) for smooth \(q\). Representatives on neighbouring cells agree on common edges.

Proof ▶

The smooth approximants converge uniformly by the sup bound (Lemma 4.10); the traces agree across edges by Lemma 4.6.

Lemma 6.3 Interpolation estimate on one cell
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.1
  • Lemma 4.9
  • Lemma 6.2

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.cell_interp_estimate

With a Bramble–Hilbert polynomial and a continuous representative \(w\), the Lagrange interpolant \(I_Tw\) satisfies \(h_T^{2i}\lvert J-I_Tw\rvert _{H^i(T)}^2\le Ch_T^{2(k+1)}\lvert J\rvert _{H^{k+1}(T)}^2\) for \(i\le 2\), and the corresponding pointwise bound.

Proof ▶

\(J-I_Tw=(J-q)-I_T(w-q)\); the second term is bounded through the nodal values of \(w-q\), using the sup bound and a Lebesgue constant of the Lagrange basis.

Lemma 6.4 Interpolation in the DG norm
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 6.3
  • Lemma 4.6
  • Definition 2.8

Lean declarations

  • ABCM.exists_interp
  • ABCM.exists_interp_normN

For \(J\in H^1_0(\Omega )\cap H^{p+1}(\Omega )\) realized by \(U\) there is \(v_h\in V_h\), continuous across edges and zero on \(\partial \Omega \), with \(N_h(U-v_h)\le Ch^p\lvert J\rvert _{H^{p+1}(\Omega )}\) and \(\lVert U-v_h\rVert _{L^2}\le Ch^{p+1}\lvert J\rvert _{H^{p+1}(\Omega )}\).

Proof ▶

Take the cellwise Lagrange interpolant of the continuous representative; the interpolants glue, so the jumps of \(U-v_h\) vanish and \(N_h\) reduces to the broken \(H^1\) and \(H^2\) parts, bounded by Lemma 6.3.
